Agreeing when payment is due and which completed work it covers

A proposal that asks for money before the work starts may not say what that money secures. If a deposit is requested, or payment is split into stages, list the tasks each payment would cover for your Woodbridge home: which ducts, vents or components, and which photographs or notes show a task is finished. Ask what happens to the amount if a section cannot be reached. Ask a responding provider to answer all of this in writing before anything is agreed.

  1. Return grilles

    Openings that pull room air back into the system.

  2. Return ducts

    Carry that air back to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The largest duct carrying air away from the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    The individual runs that split off toward each room.

  6. Supply registers

    The floor, wall or ceiling vents in each room.

Questions About Your Woodbridge Request

Once my proposal goes to Woodbridge Duct Cleaning, who is on the other end?

Each request goes to our enquiry team for review. Woodbridge Duct Cleaning is an enquiry website and not a contractor. It does no cleaning, handles no payments and has no staff, vehicles or office. Should the team pass a request on, the provider who receives it must say who they are, confirm they can attend your address and put payment terms in writing before anything is arranged.

How does the enquiry team deal with a Woodbridge duct request, and what stays open?

You settle the payment terms, coverage and written scope in writing once a responding provider has confirmed them, and until then they stay open. Our enquiry team receives your description, and any proposal you attach, for review. Sending does not choose a provider, set a price, fix a date or commit you to anything.

What should a deposit for work in my Woodbridge home be tied to?

Ask the provider to state in writing what the deposit secures, for example reserved materials or a named task, and what happens to it if the work changes or a section cannot be reached. A deposit with no named purpose is hard to match to the receipt later. Whether to pay one is your decision, made after reading the written terms.

Which finished tasks in my Woodbridge duct job should trigger a payment?

A task counts as finished when the written scope says so and a record backs it up, such as a photograph at a named register or a receipt line. Ask the provider to list the tasks in order and say which ones release a payment. Tasks that were not reached or not done should not be paid for as if they had been.

What happens to the amount if a Woodbridge duct section cannot be reached?

The written terms should say it before the work starts. Ask whether the amount for that section is removed, held back or moved to another task, and who tells you. The provider should record the section as not reached, with a reason, so the receipt shows what was done and what was left out. Do not accept a change that is only spoken aloud.

What should the receipt name after duct or vent cleaning in my home?

Ask for a receipt that names each task finished, each section left out, the date and the person who accepted the work, and any amount that changed from the proposal. Keep it with the photographs and the written scope. If something is missing, ask the provider to correct the receipt rather than relying on memory of what was said.

Can a tenant in Woodbridge agree to payment terms for duct work?

That depends on who controls the system, which a landlord, property manager or condominium corporation can tell you. Ask them before accepting any proposal, and ask the provider to name the person who authorized the work and the party who will be invoiced. Questions about a lease belong with the person who can answer them.

What if a proposal asks for payment in full before any cleaning is done?

Ask the provider why, and what the payment secures. Ask for the same proposal broken into tasks, with a payment tied to each finished and recorded one. You may also ask for a written statement of what is refunded if the work is not done. The decision stays with you, and nothing is agreed through this site.
