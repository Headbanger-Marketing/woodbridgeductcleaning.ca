Questions About the Work

Is a duct treatment justified before any deposit is paid?

It may be premature to pay for a treatment first, since a product does nothing for a moisture source that remains. Once the cause is corrected, the product, its registered use and the surfaces can be set out before payment.

  • Say what you saw, and where

    Write down the odour, staining or damp spot, which register or room it is near and when you notice it, and photograph any staining at a register. Ask the provider to establish what was observed inside the accessible ductwork, whether a moisture source exists and who would correct it, before any treatment is priced or any deposit is requested for one in your Woodbridge home.

  • Cleaning alone against a treatment

    Compare cleaning the accessible surfaces with adding a product afterwards. Ask whether the second step depends on what the first one shows, and if so, whether payment for it waits until then.

  • Product name and what the deposit secures

    Ask for the written scope to name the product, its registered use, the surfaces it would touch and what anyone living there, pets included, should do afterwards, and to say what any deposit secures if the treatment is later judged unnecessary. The receipt should list the cleaning and the treatment as separate items, say whether the product was applied at all and state no outcome such as odour removal.
