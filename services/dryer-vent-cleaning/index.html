Questions About the Work

Does one payment cover the dryer vent run to the hood?

A dryer vent can run through a wall, joist space or ceiling, and a proposal may charge for the whole route when only the first section is cleaned. If the hood end is blocked, ask whether the amount changes.

  • Trace the route and the hood end

    Note where the dryer stands, what connector you can see behind it, and where the vent ends outside, with a photograph of the hood and its flap. Ask the provider to establish how long the run is, how many bends it has and whether the outside end can be reached, since each of those may change what a payment for finished work ought to cover.

  • Connector only against the full run

    Set payment for the connector and first section beside payment for the whole route to the hood. Ask which one the proposal describes and what decides whether it is done.

  • Hood photo before the final payment

    Who moves and reconnects the dryer should be named in the written scope; a gas connection is work for appropriately qualified personnel and belongs on its own line. Ask for a photograph of the outside hood and flap taken when the run is finished, and for any blocked section in your Woodbridge home to be recorded as a finding with its own line on the receipt, not folded into the agreed amount.
